# THE STROMEX EDITORIAL BIBLE — EDITION II

### The Constitution, Operating Manual and Strategic Corpus of StromeX

**Edition II · Ratified as the governing corpus of the StromeX group · Supersedes Edition I**

---

## What this corpus is

This is not a business plan. A business plan is written to be read once, by an investor, and then filed. This corpus is written to be *consulted* — for twenty years, by employees who have not met the founder, in cities that do not yet have a StromeX office, when deciding whether to build a feature, what to charge for it, whether to enter a market, and whether a decision is consistent with who we said we were.

It is the supreme governing authority of the StromeX group. Where a roadmap, contract, pricing sheet, deck, or line of code conflicts with this corpus, this corpus wins until formally amended under the protocol in Volume I, Chapter 9.

## What changed in Edition II

Edition I described a company: an AI operating system for knowledge work, with Arabic and Islamic scholarship as first-class domains. That description was correct and remains true — it is now Volume III, Division 1, one product line among many.

Edition II describes a **group**. StromeX is an AI-powered digital transformation company that digitises, automates and modernises organisations across every sector it can serve competently and ethically. Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools is not the boundary of the vision; it is Reference Implementation №1 — the proving ground that demonstrates the method works end-to-end on a real institution with real students, real fees, real certificates and real reputational risk.

The reframing is structural, not cosmetic. Edition I asked "what should our product do?" Edition II asks "what does an institution need in order to be modern, and which of those needs can StromeX meet, at what price, with what margin, at what scale, in which country, by which year?"

## The three phases

Every date in this corpus derives from the roadmap ratified in **Volume VIII, Chapter 1**:

| Phase | Years | Name | Governing question |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | 2027–2030 | **Foundation** | *"Will this decision make StromeX stronger in 2031?"* |
| **II** | 2031–2035 | **Race** | *"How do we scale without compromising quality, trust or customer satisfaction?"* |
| **III** | 2036–2040 | **Global Scale** | *"How do we become durable enough to outlive our founders?"* |

A phase ends when its **gate** is passed (Volume VIII, Chapter 2), not when its years elapse.

## The two standing instructions

**On the corpus** (Volume IX, Chapter 10):
> Do not treat this Editorial Bible as a finished document. Treat it as a living constitution. Whenever you identify a missing capability, governance principle, product category, revenue model, technology, operational process, or strategic opportunity that aligns with StromeX's mission and long-term sustainability, propose and incorporate it with clear justification. Distinguish evidence-based recommendations from aspirational ideas, preserve internal consistency, and ensure every addition strengthens the company over decades rather than optimising for short-term gains.

**On the objective** (Volume VIII, Chapter 5):
> The primary objective of StromeX is not a valuation. It is: build products customers genuinely value, achieve world-class quality, create a trusted brand, generate sustainable cash flow, expand methodically, invest consistently in R&D, and reinvest profits wisely. A very large valuation is a possible consequence of doing those things exceptionally well over two decades — it is not the mission, and it must never become the operating target.

## Standing rules for this corpus

1. **Nothing is asserted without reasoning.** Every recommendation states why. A reader must be able to disagree with the reasoning rather than merely with the conclusion.
2. **Estimates are labelled as estimates.** Every financial figure in Volume VIII carries its assumptions inline. No number in this corpus is a promise, a forecast of record, or a guarantee. Where a range is given, the range is the honest statement and the midpoint is not more true than the edges.
3. **The corpus grows.** It is expected to expand continuously as divisions mature and opportunities are discovered. A chapter that has not been amended in twelve months is flagged for review, not assumed correct.
4. **Prices are living.** Volume III is versioned independently and reviewed quarterly (Volume III, Chapter 0). A price in a signed contract is honoured for its term regardless of later revision.
5. **We do not write fiction.** No invented customer quotes, no fabricated market research, no imaginary case studies presented as fact. Where this corpus synthesises established industry practice, it says so. Where it reasons from first principles, it says so.

## Provenance & evidence base

Edition II was drafted against the following, and every claim it makes about what the group has built traces to one of them:

**The group's own code and delivery record**
- The StromeX MVP in this repository — `apps/api` (FastAPI, multi-provider AI routing, memory, Qur'an tutor, book/PDF export), `apps/web` (Next.js), `infra`, and the Android release
- Edition I founding documents (`docs/00`–`docs/02`), the MVP architecture record (`docs/03`), and the independent audit (`docs/04`) — including the critical SSRF vulnerability found, reproduced and fixed, which is carried forward as a standing engineering rule in Volume IV §15
- The Android and authentication delivery records (`docs/05`–`docs/14`)

**The four institutional reference implementations**
- **Reference Implementation №1 — Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools** (`ahmadsulaimiy1/Sultan-`): a four-language build (English, Arabic RTL, Yorùbá, French) with ~35 unique page templates per locale; four distinct verification surfaces (certificate, identity, receipt, graduation document); a certificate generation system with documented identifier architecture, security model and signing-key deployment; digital identity and card system; finance platform; student, parent, staff, registrar, bursary, executive, founder and alumni portals; personalisation centre; push notifications; marketplace; digital campus; prospectus system; and approximately 120 institutional system, governance and design documents
- **`ahmadsulaimiy1/worldwencollege`**: a 73-table credential-and-commerce schema across 15 migrations, covering enrolment integrity, competencies, evidence, awards, credential signing, issued documents, verifying institutions, graduate identity, governance decisions, programme definition, payments, refunds, instalment plans, promo codes, multi-currency routing, corporate seats and alumni chapters
- **`ahmadsulaimiy1/al-madeenahcollege`**: a bilingual static-build system with offline service worker, verification surfaces, subset font pipeline, and document generation to PDF/DOCX
- **`ahmadsulaimiy1/My-books`**: a Next.js portal implementation covering student, staff, parent, applicant and admin roles, admissions pipeline, attendance, timetable, quizzes, finance and library

Volume III marks each catalogue line ✅ built · ◐ partial · ○ specified-not-built on this basis. The live `shroyalschools.com` site was not reachable from the drafting environment (egress blocked), so no claim is made about the current published state of that site's content — only about what is in the repository.

**External research** (cited inline where used): 2026 SaaS retention and valuation benchmarks, Nigerian Federal Ministry of Education school census data, messaging-platform user figures, and Matrix federation adoption in European public sector. Every external figure carries its source link at the point of use, per the authorship standard in Volume IX §10.2.

## The presentation layer

`presentation.src.html` in this directory is the source for the corpus's published front matter — a single self-contained page set in the StromeX brand faces (Archivo and Fraunces, inlined from `apps/web/public/fonts/`), carrying the volume index, the three-phase roadmap, the three financial scenarios, the entrenched provisions and the logo decision.

The two `__ARCHIVO__` / `__FRAUNCES__` placeholders are replaced with base64 of the variable-font files at build time. The masthead device is a guilloche rosette generated on canvas — the same mathematical line-work specified for security documents in Volume VI, Chapter 6, which is the group's signature artifact and therefore its correct visual signature.
