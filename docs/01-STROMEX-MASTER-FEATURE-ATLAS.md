# THE STROMEX MASTER FEATURE ATLAS

*Governed by the STROMEX EDITORIAL BIBLE (`00-STROMEX-EDITORIAL-BIBLE.md`). No feature below may ship in a form that violates Parts II, V, or VIII of that document.*

Every feature entry states: **Description · User Value · Technical Complexity · MVP Feasibility · Competitive Advantage · Revenue Potential · Long-Term Strategic Value · Strategic Score (1–10) · Defensibility Score (1–10) · Feasibility Score (1–10) · Phase**. Phases refer to the STROMEX 10-Year Master Roadmap: **MVP · Growth · Platform · Scale**.

Features that did not survive review (weak, undifferentiated, or unrealistic at claimed scale) are listed at the end of each category under "Rejected or Deferred," with the reason for rejection — per the Founding Directive's requirement that weak ideas be identified, not silently omitted.

## 1. Core Intelligence

**1.1 Unified Orchestrator Memory** — A single memory graph shared across every module (learning, research, writing, design), so a fact learned in a Qur'an lesson informs a later research citation. *Value*: no re-explaining yourself to the system. *Complexity*: High. *MVP*: Partial (per-module memory at MVP, unification at Growth). *Advantage*: competitors silo memory per app. *Revenue*: high retention driver, indirect revenue. *Strategic value*: this is the core moat. **Strategic 10 · Defensibility 9 · Feasibility 6 · Phase: MVP→Growth.**

**1.2 Confidence-Labeled Generation** — Every output is tagged with a visible confidence/verification level (verified / inferred / unverifiable), per Bible Part IV. *Value*: users know what to double-check. *Complexity*: Medium. *MVP*: Yes. *Advantage*: near-unique in consumer AI. *Revenue*: trust → retention → paid conversion. *Strategic value*: foundational to the whole trust thesis. **Strategic 10 · Defensibility 7 · Feasibility 8 · Phase: MVP.**

**1.3 Cross-Language Reasoning Core** — Native bilingual (Arabic/English) reasoning where neither language is a translation of the other's output. *Value*: no "translated AI" feel. *Complexity*: High. *MVP*: Yes, foundational. *Advantage*: most competitors translate English output into Arabic. *Revenue*: opens the entire MENA market. *Strategic value*: defines the brand. **Strategic 10 · Defensibility 8 · Feasibility 6 · Phase: MVP.**

**1.4 Explainable Refusals** — When StromeX declines to answer (fatwa requests, medical diagnosis, legal conclusions), it explains why and routes to a qualified human/resource, rather than a flat refusal. *Value*: trust preserved even in refusal. *Complexity*: Medium. *MVP*: Yes. *Advantage*: most AI just refuses. *Revenue*: indirect (trust). *Strategic value*: prevents reputational disaster in sensitive domains. **Strategic 9 · Defensibility 6 · Feasibility 8 · Phase: MVP.**

*Rejected: "General autonomous life-agent" (books flights, manages money without narrow scoping) — rejected as ungoverned blast radius exceeds Bible Part VIII oversight requirements at any phase before Platform.*

## 2. Research

**2.1 Verified Multi-Source Synthesizer** — Cross-corroborates claims across Tier 1/2 sources (Bible Part V) before presenting synthesis, flagging single-source or conflicting claims explicitly. *Value*: research trustworthy enough to cite in real academic work. *Complexity*: High. *MVP*: Core to MVP. *Advantage*: most AI search tools optimize for speed, not corroboration. *Revenue*: premium tier driver. *Strategic value*: differentiates from generic AI search. **Strategic 10 · Defensibility 8 · Feasibility 7 · Phase: MVP.**

**2.2 Living Literature Review** — A research document that auto-updates as new relevant sources are published, with a diff showing what changed and why. *Value*: researchers never work from a stale review. *Complexity*: High. *MVP*: Growth. *Advantage*: no mainstream tool maintains "living" documents. *Revenue*: strong institutional/enterprise upsell. *Strategic value*: high retention for academic users. **Strategic 8 · Defensibility 7 · Feasibility 5 · Phase: Growth.**

**2.3 Arabic Academic Corpus Access** — First-class retrieval over Arabic-language academic and classical scholarship, not just Google-indexed Arabic web content. *Value*: serves Arabic researchers who are chronically underserved. *Complexity*: High (corpus acquisition/partnerships). *MVP*: Growth. *Advantage*: near-zero competition in this specific niche. *Revenue*: institutional licensing. *Strategic value*: core to regional defensibility. **Strategic 9 · Defensibility 9 · Feasibility 5 · Phase: Growth.**

**2.4 Contradiction Detector** — Actively surfaces when sources disagree, rather than averaging them into a false consensus. *Value*: protects against synthesized-but-wrong conclusions. *Complexity*: Medium-High. *MVP*: Growth. *Advantage*: most tools hide disagreement. *Revenue*: trust/retention. *Strategic value*: reinforces the "wisdom" pillar of Part IV. **Strategic 8 · Defensibility 6 · Feasibility 6 · Phase: Growth.**

*Rejected: "Auto-generate novel research conclusions for publication" — rejected; violates Part II's "augmentation never substitution" principle for scholarly authorship.*
